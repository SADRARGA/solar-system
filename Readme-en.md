<h1>Solar System &amp; 3D Earth</h1>

<p><a href="README.md">فارسی</a></p>

<p>An interactive 3D simulator in a single HTML file, with two modes you can switch between at the top of the page.</p>

<h2>Mode 1: Solar System</h2>
<ul>
<li>The Sun, 8 planets, Pluto, the Moon and the asteroid belt</li>
<li>Rotation and orbital motion with real periods</li>
<li>Axial tilt of each planet</li>
<li>Click any body to zoom in and see its properties</li>
<li>Click the Sun to see star properties: core temperature, composition, age, fate and spectral classes</li>
<li>Earth: day and night, seasons, the 23.4&deg; axis with a live rotation angle, and a land/water percentage bar</li>
<li>Mount Everest and the Mariana Trench with their data</li>
<li>Flat world map with time lines and the day/night boundary</li>
<li>Pause/resume and time speed control</li>
</ul>

<h2>Mode 2: 3D Earth</h2>
<ul>
<li>Asks for the approximate time in Tehran, then calculates the clock in every time zone</li>
<li>Iran on the day side and the opposite side in night</li>
<li>Meridians, parallels, time zones and the 23.4&deg; axis on the globe</li>
<li>Young and old mountains, the shortest Tehran&harr;New York route, and quick jumps to the poles, Everest and the Mariana Trench</li>
<li>Flat map with each zone's clock and the dark night area</li>
<li>Time and day-of-year sliders</li>
<li>Educational panels: world clocks, calendars (Solar Hijri, Gregorian, Islamic, Hebrew, Chinese, Buddhist, Ethiopian), poles and navigation, lithosphere/atmosphere/hydrosphere, relief, and the internal, external and human forces that reshape the land</li>
</ul>

<h2>Run</h2>
<p>Open <code>index.html</code> in a browser. An internet connection is required, because three.js is loaded from a CDN.</p>

<h2>Windows 7</h2>
<p>Use <b>Firefox ESR 115</b>, the last Firefox version that supports Windows 7. Chrome 109 also works. Update your graphics driver if the page stays blank.</p>

<h2>Notes</h2>
<ul>
<li>The interface is in Persian.</li>
<li>Land shapes are simplified, and planet sizes and distances are scaled for visibility.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Built with</h2>
<p>HTML, JavaScript, <a href="https://threejs.org/">three.js</a></p>
